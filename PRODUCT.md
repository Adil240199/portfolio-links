# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Portfolio visitors, including people reviewing frontend work, and people using
the individual games and small utilities.

## Product Purpose

Present a collection of frontend projects that are easy to explore and use.
Each project should retain its own purpose while feeling complete, responsive,
and dependable.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and approachable. The experience should feel clear and
considered, not flashy.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered interfaces and unnecessary decoration.
- Excessive gradients, bright accents, and distracting effects.
- Overcomplicated architecture or interactions that obscure each project's
  purpose.

## Design Principles

- Make each project's primary task immediately clear.
- Preserve the original content and functionality while improving presentation.
- Use restrained color, deliberate typography, and consistent spacing.
- Keep interactions accessible, responsive, and easy to understand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard access, visible focus, semantic HTML,
adequate contrast, and reduced-motion preferences.
